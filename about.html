Prosperity, advised with integrity

Fanaka is a Kenyan wealth advisory built to be personal, transparent and focused on the long term.

The story behind the name

Fanaka means prosperity, comfort and success. It reflects our belief that good financial advice should help people sleep better, plan better and leave a stronger legacy.

Our story

Fanaka is Kiswahili for prosperity, comfort and success. Too many Kenyans earn well yet lack a clear plan for retirement, protection and growth. We bring pensions, insurance, investments and trusteeship together so that good advice is honest, understandable and within reach.
